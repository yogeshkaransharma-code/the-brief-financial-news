Secure Trust Bank reports loan book growth in third-quarter trading update

Secure Trust Bank announces its trading update for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026, highlighting loan book figures and capital updates.

Original news: 2026-10-22 · Published by The Brief: 2026-10-23 · Secure Trust Bank (issuer RNS)

Trading Performance

Secure Trust Bank PLC has released its trading update for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026. Chief Executive Officer Ian Corfield stated that the group continued to deliver against its strategic priorities with targeted lending growth and cost management execution.

Loan Book and Capital Updates

The group's continuing loan book reached £3.5 billion at the end of the third quarter, marking a 1.4% increase driven by Business Finance. Year-to-date loan book growth was 6.4%, while year-on-year growth reached 9.5%. During the period, Secure Trust Bank completed the second £5 million tranche of its £10 million share buyback programme, returning excess capital from the sale of its Consumer Vehicle Finance business to shareholders.

Digital Operations and Guidance

In retail operations, the V12 mobile app for Retail Finance was relaunched in September and recorded over 780,000 registered users. Looking ahead, the bank's full-year 2026 guidance remains unchanged, targeting net lending growth of 8-10% and a cost-income ratio of approximately 47%.
