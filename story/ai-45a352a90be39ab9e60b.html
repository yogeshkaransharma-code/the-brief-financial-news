Arbuthnot Banking Group lifts profit expectations on strong lending and wealth growth

The banking group reports positive third-quarter performance across its loan books and wealth management division, leading the board to anticipate higher full-year profit.

News: 22 October 2026 · Published: 23 October 2026 · Arbuthnot Banking Group (issuer RNS)

Trading Performance and Profit Outlook

Arbuthnot Banking Group PLC has announced that its board expects full-year profit before tax for 2026 to come in ahead of prior expectations. The group points to ongoing loan book growth across the first nine months of the year, a stable interest rate environment, and continued momentum within its wealth management business as the main drivers behind this upgrade.

Lending and Deposit Figures

Customer loans and leased assets reached two point five billion pounds at the thirty-first of September period end, marking a twelve percent increase over the preceding twelve months. Customer deposit balances finished the period at four point nine billion pounds, reflecting a ten percent rise year-on-year and a two percent increase during the third quarter alone. Specialist lending divisions performed particularly strongly, delivering twenty-six percent growth over the past twelve months.

Wealth Management Expansion

Funds under Management and Administration grew to three point zero nine billion pounds by the end of September, up fifteen percent from the start of the year and twenty-one percent higher than the same point in twenty-twenty-five. The wealth management unit turned a net profit for the quarter, aided by fees and commission from wealth planning advising clients.
