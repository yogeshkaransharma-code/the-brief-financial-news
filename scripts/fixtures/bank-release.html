Lloyds expands lending service for business customers

Lloyds says it has expanded a lending service for eligible business customers. The service gives participating businesses another way to apply for finance through the bank. Applications remain subject to credit checks, lending criteria and approval. The bank says the service supports customers who need finance for their business activities. It has not announced that every application will be accepted or that existing loan agreements will change.

Customers can use the bank's established business banking channels to obtain information about the lending service. The bank says its normal assessment process will continue to apply. The announcement describes a service expansion and does not announce an acquisition of another lender. Interest, repayment obligations and other contractual terms depend on the agreement offered to an individual customer. The announcement does not provide a forecast of future lending volumes or a date by which applicants will receive a decision. Businesses should review the terms of any lending offer before entering an agreement.
