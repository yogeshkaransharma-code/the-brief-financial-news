Aviva expands commercial insurance distribution agreement

Aviva says it has expanded its UK distribution agreement for commercial insurance. The agreement allows participating brokers to place eligible commercial risks through the insurer's existing distribution service. The insurer says the arrangement covers business customers who meet the product's underwriting criteria. Individual applications remain subject to assessment and acceptance by the insurer. The announcement does not change the contractual terms of existing insurance policies.

Participating brokers will continue to advise customers about the insurance cover available under the arrangement. The insurer says the agreement builds on its existing commercial insurance distribution service. Cover and eligibility depend on the relevant policy terms, conditions and exclusions. The announcement identifies an expanded distribution arrangement and does not announce an acquisition of the broker. It does not disclose financial terms or a forecast of future sales. Brokers should refer to the insurer's published product information for the specific risks covered.
